🔬 Scientific Facts

AI training requires massive data centers filled with GPUs and servers that consume huge amounts of electricity.

💡 Data Centers

Modern data centers consume massive amounts of electricity to power servers and keep them cool. They are responsible for about 1-2% of global electricity usage.

🤖 AI Model Training

Training large AI models requires billions of computational operations, consuming thousands of megawatt-hours of electricity. Larger models require exponentially more energy.

🌱 Carbon Footprint

Training GPT-3 may create about 515 metric tons of CO₂, while an average car produces around 55 metric tons over its lifetime.

♻️ Sustainability

Using renewable energy sources like wind and solar power can significantly reduce the environmental impact of AI training and data centers.

🌍 Greenhouse Gases

CO₂ and other gases trap heat in the atmosphere, causing global warming, melting ice, and shifting weather patterns worldwide.

🐻 Wildlife & Ecosystems

Melting Arctic ice causes polar bear habitat loss, ecosystem disruption, and food chain imbalance across many regions.

🌞 Renewable Energy Solutions

Solar-powered data centers

Solar arrays can power servers and cooling systems with clean, renewable energy.

Wind energy

Wind farms generate low-carbon electricity to reduce the overall footprint of AI training.

Energy-efficient AI systems

Smarter algorithms and optimized hardware help lower the energy needed to train models.

Reduce unnecessary energy waste

Conserving energy with server sharing and scheduling reduces total emissions.

Sustainable computing systems

Designing software and hardware for efficiency helps protect ecosystems and natural resources.
