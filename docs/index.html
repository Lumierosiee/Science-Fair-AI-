🔬 Scientific Facts

💡 Data Centers

Modern data centers consume massive amounts of electricity to power servers and keep them cool. They are responsible for about 1-2% of global electricity usage.

🤖 AI Model Training

Training large AI models requires billions of computational operations, consuming thousands of megawatt-hours of electricity. Larger models require exponentially more energy.

🌱 Carbon Footprint

The carbon footprint of AI training depends on the energy source. Renewable energy has a lower carbon intensity (~0.05 kg CO₂/kWh) versus fossil fuels (~0.5 kg CO₂/kWh).

♻️ Sustainability

Using renewable energy sources like wind and solar power can significantly reduce the environmental impact of AI training and data centers.

🌍 Greenhouse Gases

CO₂ is a major greenhouse gas that traps heat in the atmosphere, contributing to global warming and climate change. Every action has an environmental cost.

⚖️ Comparison

Training one large AI model can produce emissions equivalent to the annual carbon footprint of several human lifetimes. Efficiency improvements are crucial.
